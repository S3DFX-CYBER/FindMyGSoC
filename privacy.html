Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

FindMyGSoC has no accounts, no cookies and no advertising. Everything you save lives in your own browser, and the only thing that leaves it is the GitHub username you choose to analyse.

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version. No sign-up, no tracking cookies, no profile on a server. Your watchlist, drafts, badges and settings are stored in localStorage in your browser and never uploaded. Clearing your browser data clears all of it.

What is stored on your device

Everything below lives in your browser's localStorage for this site. None of it is transmitted to us.

KeyWhat it holds
themeWhether you chose the light or dark theme.
bookmarksThe organisations on your watchlist.
recentlyViewedOrgsThe organisations you opened most recently.
gssoc_badgesProgress towards the local badge system.
gaf_proposal_autosaveWhether proposal autosave is switched on, stored as the string true or false.
gaf_proposal_<org>The draft of the proposal you are writing for that organisation.
gaf_user_cacheThe last GitHub profile analysis, so you do not refetch it.
gaf_*Local counters for the analytics panel: visits, searches, filters, views.

Proposal drafts are stored per organisation, under their own key. They are plain text in your browser, so treat a shared device the way you would treat a shared notebook.

What leaves your browser

Three things, and only when you ask for them:

  • The GitHub username you type. It is sent to our edge function, which asks GitHub for your public profile and repository languages to rank organisations. Nothing else about you is sent, and the result is cached locally.
  • Requests for organisation data. Repository statistics and good first issues come from our edge functions, which call the public GitHub API. These requests carry no identifier for you.
  • Page performance data. On the deployed site we use Vercel Speed Insights, which records aggregate web vitals. It does not set advertising cookies and does not build a profile of you. It is disabled when you run the site locally.

Your resume text is processed in your browser and is never uploaded. If you paste it into the recommender, it stays in the page.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. There is no advertising network, no social tracker, no session recording and no fingerprinting script. The only third-party requests are the font files from Google Fonts, the three libraries loaded from jsDelivr (Markdown rendering, HTML sanitising and PDF export), and the Tailwind runtime the app page uses for its utility classes. If jsDelivr fails to serve one of those libraries, the page falls back to unpkg.com for the same file, so that host may appear in your network log instead.

Who else is involved

  • GitHub serves the public repository data and logos. Their privacy statement applies to those requests.
  • Vercel hosts the site and runs the edge functions. Server logs are kept for operational purposes, as with any host.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Your browser requests them directly.

Clearing your data

You are in control of all of it:

  • Remove individual organisations from the watchlist with the star on any card.
  • Clear everything at once from your browser's site-data settings for this domain. That removes bookmarks, drafts, badges, counters and the theme.
  • Nothing needs to be deleted on our side, because nothing was sent.

Age

FindMyGSoC is a tool for people preparing for Google Summer of Code, and is intended for students old enough to take part in the programme. It collects no personal information, so there is nothing to delete on request.

Changes to this page

If what the site stores changes, this page changes with it in the same pull request. The date at the top is the date of the last change, and the history is public in the repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or something here that does not match what you observed? Open an issue on GitHub, or report a vulnerability privately through the security policy.
